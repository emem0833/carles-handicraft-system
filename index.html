Study Background

This Digital Suggestion System for Handicrafts Using Local Materials was developed for the Municipality of Carles, Iloilo — home to Gigantes Island and rich traditions of buri weaving, bamboo craft, pandan mat-making, and shellcraft.

The system addresses challenges faced by local artisans: limited market access, weak online visibility, and lack of product documentation. Tourists can now discover authentic Carlesanon handicrafts and learn about material origins.

#CarlesdaBest – Supporting local artisans and sustainable tourism.

For Carlesanon Artisans

This system helps you:

  • Learn what crafts you can make from local materials (buri, bamboo, pandan, shells)
  • Find where to source materials using the geotagged map
  • Preserve traditional craft knowledge for future generations
  • Connect with tourists seeking authentic Carles products
